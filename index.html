THE OBJECT · LÄMPÖ

Designed to be held.
Made to be cared for.

LÄMPÖ combines a soft mink exterior with a cotton-lined removable sleeve and a separate refillable rice-and-herb insert. The pouch is intended for dry, supervised spa relaxation, subject to product safety and hygiene validation.

Educational prototype · Intended American mink (Neogale vison) · Not a medical device or independently certified heat product.

THE PASSPORT02 / 06

Information for everyone
who handles the object.

Choose a perspective. The record adapts to the information needed for use, care, repair or evaluation.

FOR THE GUEST

A quiet ritual
of comfort.

LÄMPÖ is an exploratory tactile wellness accessory. The mink exterior provides a soft surface, while a removable inner pouch contains a replaceable dry botanical filling.

01 / USE

Before use

Use only when supplied by a trained hotel operator under an approved, tested heating and hygiene protocol. The product is not approved for independent microwave heating.

02 / TOUCH

Stay comfortable

Check the temperature before skin contact. Do not use on broken or insensitive skin; stop use if uncomfortable. Never sleep with a heated insert or place it under bedding.

03 / SCENT

Choose your preference

Suggested filling: plain rice and optional dried lavender. Ask for an unscented insert if you prefer. Inform staff of known botanical sensitivities.

What is verified?
The dimensions and proposed materials are recorded below. Sourcing, production testing, heating instructions and certified product safety remain to be verified.
FOR THE HOTEL OPERATOR

Care is part of
the service.

Keep the object in hotel ownership. Its value depends on inspection, cleanable contact surfaces, safe filling replacement and documented maintenance.

01 / PROTOCOL

Heating remains on hold

No temperature, microwave wattage or heating time has been validated. Do not heat or issue as a functional heat product until a qualified person has approved a documented, product-specific procedure.

02 / HYGIENE

Inspect every cycle

Inspect lining, seams, closure, fur and filling for spills, scorch marks, odours, dampness or wear. Remove compromised components from service. Develop a hotel-grade cleaning method with a fur specialist.

03 / REFILL

Suggested dry filling

150 g plain dry rice + optionally 2 g dried lavender flowers, per insert. A preliminary recipe only: adjust quantity after fit, weight and safety tests. Do not add essential oils. Log only material changes or incidents, not every routine refill.

04 / STORAGE

Store with care

Remove the inner pouch for any proposed heating or cleaning. Keep fur away from direct heat, humidity and oils. Store the entire product dry and ventilated; allow any heated insert to cool on a non-combustible surface before storing.

Operational limit
Refillable does not mean an indefinitely reusable food filling. Replace it when damp, odorous, scorched or degraded; retire damaged inserts. A food-safe claim is not made for the filling.
FOR THE MAKER & REPAIRER

Constructed to
be understood.

The pattern below is a provisional interpretation of the prototype: mink on one exterior face, cotton behind the mink and on the sleeve back, with a detachable, refillable inner heat insert.

01 / CONSTRUCTION

Visible seams

Prefer accessible stitching and replaceable closures to adhesive bonds. Confirm seam allowance and the exact flap and closure arrangement during prototyping.

02 / REPAIR

Keep value in use

Refer fur repairs, re-tailoring and specialist cleaning to a qualified furrier. Cotton seams and damaged inserts may be repaired or replaced separately where the actual stitching makes this possible.

03 / DISASSEMBLY

Separate by component

Remove the heat insert, undo accessible closures, then carefully unpick cotton backing only where feasible without damaging the pelt leather. Document which parts cannot be separated cleanly.

Pattern status
Cutting estimates assume 1 cm seam allowances. Final sewn size, fastener specification, joining method and pattern yield remain pending physical validation.
FOR RESEARCH & ASSESSMENT

Evidence, rather
than assumptions.

This passport differentiates documented design specifications from future verification needs. It does not claim certification or a verified environmental advantage for mink.

01 / ORIGIN

Mink provenance

American mink is the intended material specification; actual species, seller, lot number, farm, country and processing information are not supplied. Saga Furs has a traceability system, but its use for this particular material is not established.

02 / CERTIFICATION

Not yet substantiated

No certificates are attached to this prototype. Farm-level WelFur/Furmark documentation or textile certifications may be added only after verification of the actual components.

03 / CIRCULARITY

A design intention

Material minimisation, repair and continued use are proposed strategies, not proof of low environmental impact. No product-specific life-cycle assessment, durability certification or measured heating performance is available.

04 / STATUS

Academic DPP

This is an educational, product-specific information prototype informed by EU digital product passport principles. It is not a registered or legally compliant EU DPP.

CARE & CONTINUITY04 / 06

A recipe for warmth.
Not a fixed recipe for life.

The botanical filling is intentionally refillable. Rather than permanently recording each rice or herb batch, the passport provides a suggested composition and a procedure for replacement.

SUGGESTED FILL · ONE INSERT

The botanical
heat pouch.

150 g
plain dry rice
+
2 g
dried lavender flowers · optional

Unscented version: rice only. No essential oils.

How to replenish

  1. 01

    Remove the inner cotton pouch completely from the mink sleeve.

  2. 02

    Inspect the pouch and closure; do not refill if scorched, damp, damaged or contaminated.

  3. 03

    Remove old dry filling, clean or replace the pouch according to its validated care instructions, and dry fully.

  4. 04

    Add the suggested quantities; do not overfill. Secure the pouch so no filling escapes.

  5. 05

    Check seams and record a replacement only when it affects component specifications or condition.

HEAT SAFETY / HOLD

This prototype has no validated heating protocol. Do not heat it in a microwave, oven or other appliance until textile, filling, closure and thermal safety tests establish written instructions. Never heat the mink shell.

THE LONGER LIFE05 / 06

From first use
to what comes next.

Circularity is a proposed service model: inspect and maintain first, repair and re-tailor wherever feasible, and separate components at end of service. No verified recycling route is claimed.

01

Use

Hotel-managed dry relaxation setting; no unsupervised heating.

02

Inspect

Check closure, wear, cleanliness, odour and damage at each use cycle.

03

Maintain

Renew dry filling; clean only under validated material-specific protocols.

04

Repair

Patch cotton, replace insert or engage a specialist furrier for mink.

05

Re-tailor

Adapt serviceable pelt panels to smaller tactile applications where suitable.

06

Separate

Remove filling and disassemble textile/fur layers as construction permits; verify local disposal routes.

Certification & sustainability disclosure

No finished-product certification has been verified. Saga Furs describes its traceability and WelFur/Furmark-related programmes, but no certified pelt lot has been assigned to this prototype. Any future claim requires the actual certificate or transaction documentation. Cotton has no verified organic, recycled-content or textile certification. A product-specific LCA has not been conducted.
